<size=175%><b>Parasitic Retribution 1.02</b></size>

<size=130%><b>The Item Rebalance Patch Notes</b></size>

This update focused on balancing the items to be in-line with the current gameplay loop as they were previously balanced along The original premise of the game was that there were only going to be 2 stages. This lead to many items residing on one of the two sides of a bell curve in strength, with some being extremely powerful (I'm looking at you ion splitter) and some feeling extremely underwhelming (I am also looking at you ice skates) and not having a unique identity to them. Many items had very similar changes, such as arbitrarily adding damage when it didn't feel necessary. Given how the game is now, the following changes have been made, with the changes between the old and new versions being shown below:

<b>Explosive Bullets</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous Behavior</b></color>

• On first pickup, enable explosive bullets trait.

• Increase bullet radius by 1 unit.

• Set the explosion radius to 52.5% of the main bullet's radius.

• Increase bullet damage by 5. Base Damage is 10.

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• On first pickup, enable explosive bullets trait.

• Set the explosion radius to 52.5% of the main bullet's radius. This will update with other items.

• Increase explosion's percentile damage by 10%.

• The damage of the explosion is a percent of the bullets total damage. For example, if the bullet does 10 damage and the percentile is 20%, then the explosion will do 2 damage.

<color=#64B5F6><b>Developer Commentary</b></color>

This is both a bug fix and a change as previously, the percentile was never updated from the base value of 0, therefore the explosions would do 0% of the weapons base damage, or 0 damage. This reverts that problem and still provides a damage increase as the explosions can become very powerful very quickly if used in tandem with other items. They are also balanced in the fact that they do apply self damage as well, which can become problematic in the tight corridors of some rooms. This item may be subject to change in the future.

<b>Hazmat Suit</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous Behavior</b></color>

• Upon first pickup, the player permanently became immune to poison damage.

• The player's health was fully restored.

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• The player is given 15 hazard resistance.

• The player is given 5 armor.

<color=#64B5F6><b>Developer Commentary</b></color>

Poison Immunity is being removed as it made Hazmat suit one of the most powerful items in the game. Combined with the lack of ways to restore health, grabbing this early on would trivialize the game very quickly. To keep the item with the idea of being a defensive item, the immunity was replaced with a stacking resistance and armor stat on the player. The formula used for calculating damage now is: Damage taken * 100/(100 + armor), and the same for hazard resistance. This way it provides a similar benefit while not making it too powerful.

<b>Ion Splitter</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous Behavior</b></color>

• Increases Player's multishot by 2.

• Increases Player's spread by 15 degree's (?)

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• Increases Player's multishot by 2.

• Increases Player's spread by 0.5 units.

<color=#64B5F6><b>Developer Commentary</b></color>

This change is accompanied by a overarching change to the multishot stat and the spread stat. Before the player would have 1 multishot stat base, with multishot dictating how many shots the player fired at once. Now, at base, multishot starts at 0. Multishot now dictates how many extra shots the gun shoots per trigger pull. Furthermore. Each bullet from a multishot proc is 50% smaller and deals 25% of the original damage, with this effect applying to modifiers such as trump card and backfire as well.

Simultaneously, the "Spread" system has been changed to be more consistent as well and easier to modify. In its previous iteration, the theoretical form of spread was an angle which would make an increasing cone of inaccuracy around the original shot. However, in practice, there were many odd occurrences that would occur, causing the cone to only apply in certain locations, such above, below, or behind the original shot depending on the cursor's location. In its new form, when accounting for spread, a random point is selected from inside a circle based at the cursor's location when a shot is fired, creating the same cone shape as before. With this in mind, the spread stat now increases the radius of that circle, increasing the range of possible points from which the game can attempt to shoot at.
