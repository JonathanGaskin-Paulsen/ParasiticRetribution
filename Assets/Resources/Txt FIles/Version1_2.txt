<size=130%><b>The Item Rebalance Part 2</b></size>

This update was the result of many "under the hood" changes to the game's code, more specifically, the Item system. Functionally, the items work almost identically to how they did before in terms of how they are obtained and what they do, but I now have more creative freedom on what I can do with them. With that being said, items now differentiate between the base stats of the player and modifications to the base stats. Here is an example:

• The base movement speed of the player is 8 units.

• The New Dash Functionality doubles the player's speed during the dash, being a value derived from the base value.

• Ice Skates increases the players base movement speed by 2 units per item. Therefore, the dash would now use the new modified base stats value of 10 rather than 8 when calculating the dash speed

I hope this makes sense, and each item will make it clear which way it has modified the value. Outside of this, there have been a few changes to some mechanics of the game, all of which are detailed below.

<b>Dash</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous Behavior</b></color>

• During Dash, player becomes intangible.

• Dash length base amount was .1 seconds.

• Dash Cooldown at base was .75 seconds

• Base dash speed was 40, set to 20 with Accelerant.

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• During Dash, player is given 300 Armor. This roughly equals 75% damage reduction if the player has 0 armor.

• Base dash duration is now .3 seconds.

• Dash Cooldown is now equal to 5x the modified dash duration. This means that the base dash cooldown is now 1.5 seconds.

• Dash speed is now set to twice the players base speed. Since the player's base speed is 8, the base dash speed is now 16.

<color=#FF9800><size=130%><b>Item Changes</b></size></color>

Now for the Items. As stated, almost all items are functionally the same aside from some number adjustments, just with some different under the hood code.

Note: There is two different type of math at use here: Additive vs Multiplicative modifiers. I will give a brief example of the math on how they will work when I refer to them in the notes.

Example case: something is reduced by 5% and the base amount is 20

Additive = 20 - (20 x .05% x (number of stacks))

Multiplicative = 20 * .95 ^ (Number of stacks)

<b>Explosive Bullets</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous behavior</b></color>

• On first pickup, enable explosive bullets trait.

• Set the explosion radius to 52.5% of the main bullet's radius. This will update with other items.

• Increase explosion's percentile damage by 10%.

• The damage of the explosion is a percent of the bullets total damage. For example, if the bullet does 10 damage and the percentile is 20%, then the explosion will do 2 damage.

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• When an enemy is hit, a small explosion is made.

• The damage of the explosion is equivalent to 10% (Additive) of the player's base damage.

• The radius of the explosion is set to 1 + .1 *(number of item) Units.

• Removed Self Damage.

• Bullets only Explode on enemy contact

<color=#64B5F6><b>Developer Commentary</b></color>

Again, the item is functionally the same, just with some small tweaks.

<b>BackFire Changes</b>

<color=#BD2000><b>Previous Behavior</b></color>

• On first pickup, enable the backfire feature.

• Increase gun damage by 1. Base Damage is 10.

<color=#4CAF50><b>New Behavior</b></color>

• When the player takes damage, they will spawn a damaging aura around themself.

• This aura does damage to all enemies in it every half a second.

• The damage value of the aura is half of the players base damage (static).

• The aura last 5 + 3*(number of stacks) seconds and has a static 10 second cooldown after ending.

<color=#64B5F6><b>Developer Commentary</b></color>

A rework to backfire felt necessary as the perk felt very niche, but I think that the new perk fits the name still, just in a different way.

     Again, very niche bug that only happened in very specific cases, but a bug nonetheless
